More About the Countries

France

France is a Western European nation, the European Union's largest country, known for its rich history, diverse culture, and significant global influence in art, fashion, and cuisine. A republic with its capital in Paris, France is a major economic and political power, serving as a permanent member of the UN Security Council and a founding member of the European Union.


Italy

Italy is a boot-shaped country in Southern Europe, famous for its rich history, diverse geography including the Alps and Apennines, and vibrant culture encompassing art, food, and fashion


Brazil

Brazil, the largest country in South America, is a federation with a Portuguese-speaking population, a rich cultural heritage, and a market economy. It is known for its vast Amazon Rainforest and diverse wildlife, while its capital is Brasília and its largest city is São Paulo.
